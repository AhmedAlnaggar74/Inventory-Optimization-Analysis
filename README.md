# 📦 Inventory Optimization & Stockout Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a data-driven approach to inventory management, safety stock optimization, and stockout risk evaluation. Built using **Power BI**, the dashboard helps supply chain managers identify critical inventory bottlenecks, monitor high-risk SKUs, and minimize overall holding and shortage costs.

---

## 📊 Key Business Metrics (KPIs)
- **Total Order Quantity:** Tracks total units ordered across all categories.
- **Total Inventory Cost:** Combined evaluation of Holding Costs, Ordering Costs, and Shortage/Stockout Costs.
- **Average Safety Stock:** Measures required safety buffer levels to avoid stockouts.
- **Average Reorder Point:** Identifies optimal replenishment trigger points.
- **Critical SKUs Alert Count:** Dynamic tracking of items requiring immediate reordering.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX measures, dynamic dashboard design.
- **Power Query (ETL):** Data cleansing, type transformations, and schema structuring.
- **DAX:** Custom measures for KPI metrics and conditional alert logic.
- **Dataset Source:** Kaggle (Inventory & Stockout Optimization).

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
1. **Cost Drivers:** Holding costs account for a significant portion of total inventory expenses across high-value categories.
2. **Lead Time Bottlenecks:** Specific product lines face extended supplier lead times, increasing safety stock requirements.
3. **Actionable Alerts:** Dynamic monitoring allows proactive replenishment before shortage thresholds are reached.
